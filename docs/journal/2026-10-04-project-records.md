# 2026-10-04 · Project decision records

### D-164 · Scope the journal to project decisions

- **Context:** Contributors need a traceable record of product and engineering decisions, their rationale and observed outcomes.
- **Decision:** Use a project-focused journal format and align WF6 with it.
- **Why:** Requirements, alternatives, evidence and verification explain a change's effect on the project.
- **Action / outcome:** Updated the journal guide and WF6, and narrowed earlier entries to their project outcomes. Existing decision IDs and technical records remain stable; removed IDs are not reused. Verification is recorded in the PR handoff.
