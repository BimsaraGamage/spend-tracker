- **Decision:**
  - `claude-review.yml` runs only on non-draft PRs that people open from branches in this repository.
  - It skips with a notice until a `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN` or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` secret exists.

  - It uses the workflow's `GITHUB_TOKEN`, so no extra app needs installing.
  - Its tools are limited to reading code and posting review comments. The prompt runs the R1–R6 procedure.

  (autopilot)

- **Accepted risk:** At zizmor's auditor level, the credential triggers `secrets-outside-env`. A main-only environment can't work here, because review runs execute on PR branches, never on `main`. The credential stays a repository secret.
  - **Mitigations:** it only works for the AI service; the workflow ignores fork and bot PRs; and it can be revoked at once.
- **Lesson:** A scanner finding feeds a decision; it doesn't make it. When a finding can't be fixed meaningfully, record why, and what limits the damage.
